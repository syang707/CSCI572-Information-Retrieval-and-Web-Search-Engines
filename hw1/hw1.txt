DuckDuckGo performed worse than Google based on the search result similarity measured across all 100 queries. The average percent overlap was 13.1%, which means that only about 13.1% of the top 10 Google results were also found in DuckDuckGo's results on average. The average Spearman coefficient was -5.622, indicating that the ranking order of the overlapping results was generally different from Google's ranking. Therefore, DuckDuckGo performed worse than Google in terms of both the percentage of overlapping results and the similarity of the ranking order.
